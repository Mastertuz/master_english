Hotel check-in

Закрепляем Present Perfect, Past Simple, going to, hotel vocabulary и понимание диалога на ресепшене.

1. Grammar: Present Perfect or Past Simple

В этом блоке нужно решить, говорим ли мы об опыте без точного времени или о конкретном моменте в прошлом. Если есть ever/never/before - чаще нужен Present Perfect: have/has + V3. Если есть yesterday, last night, last year - нужен Past Simple. Выберите вариант или впишите недостающую часть предложения.

Впишите: ___ in a hotel abroad?

I ___ a room last night.

Впишите: I ___ a hotel key card before.

Впишите: Yesterday I ___ a deposit. (pay)

2. Plans and polite requests

Здесь нужно тренировать фразы для реального разговора в отеле. Для планов используйте be going to + verb. Для вежливой просьбы используйте would like или could I have. Впишите полную пропущенную фразу, а не одно слово, если пропуск большой.

Впишите: I ___ at 3 p.m.

I ___ a room away from the street.

Впишите: ___ the Wi-Fi access code, please?

3. Listening: hotel check-in

Нажмите Play, прослушайте диалог 2 раза и ответьте на вопросы по содержанию. Транскрипцию откройте только после попытки ответить самостоятельно.

Checking in at the Hotel
Показать транскрипцию аудио
Angelo: Hello sir, how may I help you? Tony: Yes, I have a reservation for tonight. Angelo: Okay, may I have your name please? Tony: My name is Tony McKay. Angelo: Hello Mr. McKay. You reserved a single room for four nights? Tony: Yes, that's correct. Angelo: May I see some form of ID please? Tony: Yes, here you are. Angelo: Thanks Mr. McKay. I need a credit card to put on file in case you use the mini bar. Tony: Okay, here you are. Angelo: Thanks. Would you like to upgrade to a deluxe room for only twenty dollars per night? Tony: What does the deluxe room include? Angelo: The deluxe room includes breakfast, and you can use the gym. Tony: That sounds great. Yes, I'll upgrade. Angelo: Okay, can you please sign here? Tony: Yes, no problem. Thanks a lot. Angelo: You are in room 2407. Please take the elevators on the right. Breakfast is from six to nine a.m. Tony: That's great. Thank you very much.

4A. Reading: checking into a hotel

Прочитайте текст и выполните задания. В тексте 12 предложений.

Anna arrives at a hotel after a long flight. She goes to the reception desk and gives her passport. The receptionist asks if she has a reservation. Anna says that she booked a single room online. The receptionist finds her booking and asks for a deposit. Anna pays by card and asks for the Wi-Fi access code. She would like a quiet room because she has an online meeting in the morning. The receptionist gives her a key card and explains where the elevator is. Her room is on the fifth floor at the end of the corridor. Breakfast is included from seven to ten. Anna thanks the receptionist and asks about sightseeing near the hotel. The receptionist gives her a small map and wishes her a comfortable stay.
