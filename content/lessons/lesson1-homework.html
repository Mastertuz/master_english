3. Listening: customs questions

Нажмите Play и прослушайте диалог 2 раза. Первый раз поймайте общую ситуацию. Второй раз отвечайте на вопросы.

English for most common airport customs questions
Транскрипция
Officer: Hello madam. Can I see your passport please? Passenger: Passport, yes. Officer: I'm going to ask you some questions. Are you travelling alone? Passenger: Yes, I'm alone. Officer: Is it your first time in Canada? Have you ever visited Canada before? Passenger: No, first time. Officer: Are you travelling for work, study or leisure? Passenger: I don't understand. Officer: I mean, are you here to study, work or for fun, leisure time? Passenger: Oh, to see my brother. Officer: Leisure then. Where will you be staying, in a hotel or someone's home? Passenger: My brother's house. Officer: What is the duration of your stay? Passenger: Two weeks. Officer: Do you have medical insurance? Passenger: Yes, I have insurance. Officer: Great. Welcome to Canada.

Who asks the questions?

How is the woman travelling?

Has she visited Canada before?

What is the purpose of her trip?

Where will she be staying?

What is the duration of her stay?

What document or protection does the officer ask about at the end?

4A. Reading: airport conversations

Прочитайте текст и выполните задания на понимание. В тексте 12 предложений.

Maria arrives at the airport in Toronto in the morning. She feels excited because it is her first trip to Canada. At passport control, the officer asks to see her passport. He also asks if she is travelling alone. Maria says that she is alone and that she is visiting her brother. The officer asks if she is travelling for work, study or leisure. Maria does not understand the word leisure at first. The officer explains that leisure means fun time or free time. Maria says she will stay at her brother's house. She plans to stay for two weeks. She has medical insurance and shows the document on her phone. After the questions, the officer smiles and welcomes her to Canada.

Where does Maria arrive?

Who is Maria visiting?

Which word means free time or fun time?

How long does she plan to stay?

What does she show on her phone?
